There's a Dilbert for that!

Describe an absurd workplace situation and find the Dilbert strips that match.

Open index.html in Chrome or Safari. Nothing to install, and it works offline.


SHOWING THE STRIPS

Search works without the strips, but showing them needs your own copy of the
archive, which this app does not include. You can look for one by searching for
"download dilbert archive". Check that you are allowed to use any copy you find.

1. Download and unpack the archive anywhere you like. The app expects year
   folders (1989, 1990 and so on) directly inside the archive folder.

2. Chrome: open Settings at the bottom of the app, set Archive location to that
   folder, for example /Users/you/Dilbert, and click Test location.

3. Safari: Safari only lets the page load images from its own folder, so move
   all the app files inside the archive folder, next to the year folders. The
   app then finds the strips by itself.

4. If the strips still do not show, the folders or filenames probably differ
   from what the app expects. Run index-archive.command once, which comes with
   the app. It finds each strip by the YYYY-MM-DD in its filename and writes
   archive-index.js next to itself. Mac and Linux only.
